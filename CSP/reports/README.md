# Reporting instruments

Use these documents to prepare and review a task–competency package. They are reusable guides, not completed application reports.

| Instrument | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Instructional Entity Analysis Report](CSP-Instructional-Entity-Analysis-Report.md) | Phase 1A: describe the instructional entity, required actions, products, conditions, and setting. |
| [Phase 1 Report Template](CSP-Report-Template.md) | Phases 1A–1B: enumerate knowledge and objectives and formulate the candidate specifications and their intended uses. |
| [Review Report Template](CSP-Review-Report-Template.md) | Phase 2: identify the material examined, review concerns, recommendations, adopted changes, and unresolved issues. |
| [Semantic Structuring Guide](CSP-Structuring.md) | Phase 3: compare definitions, contextual associations, and relationships across the catalog. |

## Applying the instruments

1. Identify the instructional material and its version.
2. Compare the required performance with available definitions before introducing identifiers.
3. Document reviewer roles and scope of contribution without inventing individual review sequences absent from the records.
4. Distinguish recommendations from implemented adjustments.
5. Consolidate the applicable definition, task association, and justification; retain references to earlier states where available.

The documents retain terminology from their development. “Validated” in a guide or historical report denotes the documented review outcome, not independent proof of educational validity. The consolidated process distinguishes required skill levels from attributed proficiency and intended evidence from collected assessment evidence.
