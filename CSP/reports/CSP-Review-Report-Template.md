# CSP Phase 2 — Expert Review Template Report

### Purpose

This report documents the review of CSP artifacts by collaborators with relevant domain, pedagogical, or competency-modeling expertise. The review examines the quality of the artifacts produced in Phase 1, especially the clarity, consistency, and alignment of the instructional entity analysis and the resulting preliminary competency specifications.

The report records technical and pedagogical feedback on CSP documents, models, and specifications. It does not evaluate learner performance or collect learner assessment evidence. Reviewers act as artifact reviewers, and this template does not request personal, sensitive, or behavioral information about them. The applicable research ethics procedures, including the classification of reviewer participation, are documented separately from this report.

### 2. Review Criteria

The artifact review is guided by the following criteria:

- **Knowledge granularity:** Whether the selected knowledge components are described at an appropriate level of detail.
- **Terminological consistency:** Whether terminology and controlled vocabulary are used consistently across competency titles, descriptions, knowledge components, skills, and dispositions.
- **Bloom-level alignment:** Whether the selected action verbs and cognitive levels adequately express the expected cognitive performance.
- **Textual clarity:** Whether competency titles, descriptions, and supporting explanations are clear, precise, and unambiguous.
- **Knowledge relevance:** Whether the listed knowledge components are relevant, applicable, and correctly related to the instructional entity requirements.
- **Instructional alignment:** Whether the competency specifications are coherent with the learning objectives, expected outcomes, evidence descriptions, and assessment conditions documented for the instructional entity.

### 5. Conclusion

The expert review supports the refinement of CSP artifacts by examining their conceptual clarity, pedagogical coherence, terminological consistency, and alignment with the instructional entity. The incorporated revisions result in a validated competency specification, while the documented recommendations and decisions constitute the Review Feedback Report. These outputs provide the basis for semantic structuring in Phase 3 and requirement evidence for OntoKSD traceability and refinement provenance.
