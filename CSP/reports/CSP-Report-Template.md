# CSP Phase 1 (A & B) Template Report

## 1. Introduction

This document provides a template for reporting Phases 1A and 1B of the Competency Specification Process (CSP). Together, these phases establish the contextual and pedagogical basis for preliminary competency specification.

- **Phase 1A — Instructional Entity Analysis**

  Analyzes the structural, contextual, and pedagogical components of an instructional entity, such as a PBL scenario, case study, simulation, or project. This phase documents its title, description, expected learner actions or solution process, acquisition context, expected outcomes, target learner profile, and proficiency assumptions. The resulting analysis provides the basis for competency specification.

- **Phase 1B — Competency Specification**

  Uses the instructional entity analysis to identify relevant knowledge components and explicit or implicit learning objectives. Based on this analysis, the competency author develops preliminary competency specifications that document knowledge components, Bloom-aligned cognitive skills, behavioral dispositions, acquisition context, and proficiency expectations.

By integrating these phases, the template supports a coherent progression from understanding the instructional entity to specifying what learners are expected to mobilize and demonstrate. Within this thesis, the resulting artifacts support consistency and traceability and provide requirement evidence for OntoKSD. They describe intended competencies and do not constitute evidence of learner performance.

## 2. Objective

This report documents how Phases 1A and 1B of the CSP are applied to a specific instructional entity by:

- enumerating the required knowledge components;
- identifying explicit and implicit learning objectives;
- specifying the knowledge–skill pairings associated with effective task performance; and
- documenting the behavioral dispositions relevant to the competency specifications.

## 3. Report Structure

All CSP Phase 1 reports follow the same structure.

### 3.1. Instructional Entity Analysis

**Purpose:** Provide a structured overview of the instructional entity.

**Include:** The nature and description of the task, its expected outcomes, expected learner actions or solution process, acquisition context, target learner profile, deliverables, and proficiency assumptions.

**How:** Use the Instructional Entity Analysis Report template to guide the description and maintain consistency across reports.

This analysis provides the basis for identifying the relevant knowledge, skills, and dispositions in the following sections.

### 3.2. Knowledge Enumeration

Identify the theoretical and practical knowledge required by the instructional entity. When applicable, use recognized references, such as the ACM Computer Science Body of Knowledge (CS2013 or CS2023), or other domain-relevant standards to support consistent terminology.

### 3.3. Learning Objectives Identification

Identify and articulate the explicit and implicit learning objectives derived from the instructional entity.

### 3.4. Competency Definition

Define the preliminary competencies associated with the instructional entity using the following standardized structure.

#### Title and Description

- Provide a clear, focused title and a concise description of the competency, its purpose, and the expected performance.

#### Acquisition Context

- Describe the instructional or application context in which the competency is expected to be developed or mobilized, such as a laboratory exercise, project, or simulation.

#### Proficiency Scale

- Specify the performance levels associated with the competency expectations, using the proficiency assumptions documented for the instructional entity.

#### Knowledge Components

- List the theoretical and practical knowledge required to perform the competency. When applicable, align the terminology with recognized frameworks, such as the ACM classification systems or other domain taxonomies.

#### Behavioral Dispositions

- Identify the dispositions relevant to the competency based on the CC2020 dispositions.

#### Knowledge–Skill Mapping

- Associate each knowledge component with the relevant cognitive action using Bloom’s Revised Taxonomy:
  - **Cognitive level:** for example, Apply, Analyze, or Create.
  - **Action verb:** selected to express the expected cognitive action at the corresponding level.

### 3.5. Summary Table

Provide a concise matrix summarizing each competency title and its associated knowledge components, skills, and dispositions.

## 4. Conclusion

Phases 1A and 1B establish the contextual and pedagogical basis for preliminary competency specification. Phase 1A documents the instructional entity, and Phase 1B uses that analysis to specify the relevant knowledge components, cognitive skills, and behavioral dispositions. As a result, each preliminary competency is:

- grounded in the instructional entity;
- associated with relevant theoretical and practical knowledge;
- expressed through explicit knowledge–skill pairings and performance expectations; and
- aligned with the intended learning outcomes.

The preliminary competency specifications produced in Phase 1 provide the input for Phase 2 — Expert Review. In Phase 2, reviewers examine and refine their clarity, relevance, scope, and consistency before the validated specifications proceed to semantic structuring in Phase 3.
