# Task01 — Expert Review of the Second CSP Iteration

## Review Scope

This review examines the additional refinement recorded after the initial Task01 specification, expert review, and implemented adjustments. Its scope is limited to the reassociation of Task01 with the more specific competency `Develop problem solutions using Finite State Machines`.

The previously validated competency set was used as the basis for this review. The instructional entity, proficiency assumptions, and other competency specifications were not reopened.

## Review Findings

The broader competency originally associated with Task01 was:

> Develop problem solutions using Automata

Although technically valid, the term *automata* may include models beyond the instructional scope of Task01, such as pushdown automata, Turing machines, and linear bounded automata.

The competency defined during the CSP application to Task03 provides a more precise representation of the Task01 modeling requirement:

> Develop problem solutions using Finite State Machines

The review determined that associating this existing competency with Task01 improves terminological precision and alignment with the finite-state model required by the task. The broader competency remains in the repository for contexts requiring its wider scope.

This refinement adds a more precise competency association without removing or redefining the broader competency. It does not change the instructional entity, expected outcomes, proficiency assumptions, or assessment conditions. It also does not reintroduce the equivalence and transformation objectives removed during the initial expert review.

## Review Decision

Approved.

The additive association of Task01 with C06 — `Develop problem solutions using Finite State Machines` — is accepted. C01 remains unchanged, as documented in `Task01-CSP-Report-v2.md`.

## Key Outcomes

1. The revisions implemented after the initial expert review remain approved.
2. C06 is accepted as an additional finite-state-specific competency associated with Task01.
3. C01 remains associated with its original Task01 specification and is not redefined, replaced, or removed.
4. The second iteration is recorded as an additive refinement based on competency reuse, not as a repetition of the complete CSP workflow.

## Conclusion

The second review confirms that C06 represents the Task01 modeling scope more precisely while C01 preserves the broader formulation. The approved additive refinement improves consistency between the task, its validated knowledge components, and the competency repository.
