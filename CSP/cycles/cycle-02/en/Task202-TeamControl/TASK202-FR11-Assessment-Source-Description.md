# Task202 — sources relevant to individual judgment traceability (FR11)

The records below concern the historical delivery of Task202 (Team
Control) in Cycle 2. They provide different kinds of evidence.

| Source | Author and scope | Information relevant to traceability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tutor group-review records | Tutors. Observations at group level during development of the solution. | Document group work and dispositions observed at that level. The level of attribution is the group. |
| Individual assessment questionnaire | Students. Self-assessments and peer assessments of individual contribution. | The form identifies the respondent and the person being assessed in its contribution blocks. Items concern participation, preparation for discussions, relevant questions and answers, initiative, listening and respect for others' opinions, and team organization and consensus. The form also includes feedback about the task and tutoring. |
| Official results and supporting product records | Historical assessment documentation. Scores and their sources, with team-level artifacts where applicable. | Provide a basis for tracing process and product scores to assessment records and distinguishing shared products from individual participation. |

The evaluated historical encoding represents questionnaire submissions
through documentary references and, where the linkage was established,
participation records. This supports tracing the documented submission
to its assessment occasion. The correspondence between an individual
response and a specific OntoKSD competence or constituent, with its
applicable criterion and supporting evidence, remains unverified in the
evaluated artifact. FR11 therefore remains partially supported. The
questionnaire documents student assessments, while the tutor records
provide observations at group level.

This description summarizes the instruments and their evidential
scope. Original reviews, completed questionnaires, and grade records
are held separately in the restricted source collection.
