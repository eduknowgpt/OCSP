# Ciclo de Vida da Competência C07 - *Develop Problem-Solving Solutions Using Turing Machines*


## 1. Definição Original — *CSP-Report (Ciclo 1)*

**Origem:**
A competência C07 emerge diretamente da análise da tarefa *Traffic Control*, que exige:

* interpretação de requisitos reais (DERBA),
* tradução para especificações formais,
* construção de um sistema computacional completo baseado em **Turing Machines**.

**Evidência instrucional:**

* necessidade de classificar, contar e decidir com base em dados de sensores;
* impossibilidade de resolver o problema apenas com reconhecimento ou teste de modelos.

➡️ **Resultado:** identificação da necessidade de uma competência de **desenvolvimento de soluções formais**, não apenas de compreensão.



### Primeira Especificação (CSP Phase 1B: Competency Authoring)


### Competency Title
    Develop Problem-Solving Solutions Using Turing Machines

### Textual Description  
Design and implement a Turing Machine that processes and classifies vehicle data, transforming user requirements into a functional model. Validate the model through simulation and ensure alignment with practical constraints.


### Knowledge Specification
The following knowledge areas are critical for this competency:  

- **Turing Machines (nmT):**
    - Essential to achieving the overall objective, this knowledge involves understanding and applying the principles of Turing Machines to model the solution for traffic categorization and analysis.

- **Requirements Analysis:**
    - Necessary to accurately identify and formalize the needs of DERBA technicians, ensuring that system requirements are well understood and properly implemented.

- **Analytical and Critical Thinking (FPK):**
    - Fundamental for planning and developing the solution, enabling students to analyze available information, evaluate alternative strategies, and propose approaches that balance accuracy and efficiency.

### Disposition Specification

The analysis of the **Traffic Control on Aratu Road*** task highlights key behavioral dispositions required to solve the problem and deliver an effective solution. The main dispositions include:

**Collaborative**
- Work closely with team members to design and integrate all parts of the system, share insights, and ensure a cohesive solution.

**Responsible**
- Demonstrate commitment to the quality of the solution, meeting deadlines, and ensuring that DERBA's technical requirements are fully addressed.

**Proactive**
- Anticipate potential implementation challenges, propose improvements, and adapt the computational model to increase the accuracy of vehicle classification.

**Inventive**
- Explore different Turing Machine variants and their applicability to traffic control, analyzing the most effective ways to process sensor data from the highway.

**Creative**
- Develop innovative solutions to enhance the nighttime traffic data analysis, allowing the system to become more efficient and responsive to infrastructure management needs.

### Knowledge-Skill Pairing

This step maps **knowledge areas to the corresponding skills** required to successfully demonstrate competency in this task.  

#### Mapping Knowledge to Skills

To achieve this competency, students must demonstrate the ability to: 

- **Apply** *Analytical and Critical Thinking* along with knowledge of *Turing Machines* to develop a computational solution that processes and classifies traffic data. This includes modeling sensor behavior and vehicle categorization within the formal structure of a Turing Machine.

- **Construct** *Turing Machines* to represent and simulate the logic of counting and categorizing vehicles, ensuring that the model correctly processes the data and delivers an efficient solution to the problem.

- **Apply** *Requirements Analysis* to correctly identify and specify the essential requirements of the system. Students must elicit DERBA’s needs, interpret sensor data, and define criteria for traffic analysis, ensuring that user requirements are translated into practical functionalities in the developed solution.


#### Bloom’s Taxonomy Alignment

To ensure a structured and progressive learning approach, each knowledge component is aligned with Bloom’s Revised Taxonomy, providing a clear framework for competency assessment.

- **MTuring Machines - Create**
- Create level is used to assess students’ ability to employ the concept of Turing Machines in modeling and developing a solution to the traffic control problem.

- **Requirements Analysis - Apply**
- Apply level is used to evaluate whether students can interpret and structure system requirements based on the problem’s needs.

- **Analytical and Critical Thinking (FPK) - Apply**
- Apply level is selected to assess students’ ability to break down the problem into manageable components and develop effective strategies for solving it.

To provide clarity on competency expectations, the following verb annotations define the required actions:  

**Develop** a formal Turing Machine: states, tape alphabet, head movements, and transition function.
**Construct** and simulate the TM to model realistic traffic light or vehicle queue behaviors.
**Invent** enhancements to demonstrate adaptability and originality.

## 2. Revisões — *CSRP-Report (Ciclo 1 — Revisão por Especialistas)*

**Principais achados do CSRP:**

* Identificação de **conteúdo teórico não operacional**, em especial a inclusão da **Church–Turing Thesis**, que não é mobilizada na resolução efetiva da tarefa e, portanto, não contribui diretamente para a demonstração da competência;
* Necessidade de **reorientar a especificação da competência para a execução do modelo**, enfatizando a construção, validação e funcionamento da Turing Machine, em detrimento de discussões metateóricas;
* Confirmação da **adequação do verbo nuclear (*Develop*) e do nível cognitivo (*Create*, segundo a Taxonomia de Bloom Revisada)**, coerentes com a natureza produtiva e construtiva da tarefa.

**Decisão técnica:**

* **Refinar os conhecimentos associados**, removendo elementos não exercitados pela tarefa e alinhando-os estritamente aos requisitos operacionais e instrucionais;
* Reforçar explicitamente o caráter de **competência produtiva e aplicada**, centrada na modelagem formal e na resolução de problemas por meio de Turing Machines.






## 3. Alterações Implementadas — *CSP-Adjustments*

**Ajustes implementados:**

* migração para **CS2013** (maior granularidade);
* substituição de *Requirements Analysis* por *Requirements Engineering*;
* remoção de conhecimentos não exercitados;
* alinhamento fino entre conhecimento, habilidade e verbo.


### Competency C07 Specification (CSP-Adjustments) 

### Competency Title
    Develop Problem-Solving Solutions Using Turing Machines

### **Textual Description — Revised**

Design and implement **complete problem-solving solutions** using **Turing Machines** as a formal model of computation. This competency involves **eliciting and structuring system requirements**, translating them into **precise machine-level specifications**, and **constructing executable Turing Machines** that correctly solve well-defined computational problems.

Learners must be able to **define states, tape alphabets, transition functions, and halting conditions** in accordance with the problem specification, ensuring **logical correctness, completeness, and functional adequacy** of the resulting model. Solutions should be validated through execution or simulation, demonstrating that the machine behaves as intended for all relevant input cases.

This competency emphasizes the **practical application of formal reasoning and computational modeling**, focusing on the **productive use of Turing Machines** to represent, implement, and validate solutions to complex problems, rather than on abstract theoretical discussion. It prepares students to employ Turing Machines as an **operational problem-solving tool** within the broader context of theoretical computer science and algorithmic analysis.



## **Knowledge Specification**

The following knowledge areas are essential for demonstrating this competency:

* **Turing Machines (nmT):**
  Knowledge of the formal structure and operational semantics of Turing Machines, including states, tape alphabet, transition functions, head movement, and halting conditions, as required to construct executable computational models.

* **Requirements Engineering:**
  Ability to elicit, analyze, and formalize system requirements, translating real-world problem constraints into precise specifications suitable for implementation using formal computational models.

* **Analytical and Critical Thinking (FPK):**
  Capacity to decompose complex problems, identify relevant variables and constraints, and reason systematically about solution strategies within a formal modeling context.



## **Disposition Specification**

The *Traffic Control* task requires behavioral dispositions that support effective formal modeling and problem solving:

* **Collaborative** — work constructively with peers during analysis, modeling, and validation phases;
* **Responsible** — ensure correctness, completeness, and adherence to problem specifications;
* **Proactive** — anticipate modeling challenges and propose improvements to the solution;
* **Inventive** — explore alternative modeling strategies within the expressive power of Turing Machines;
* **Creative** — design efficient and coherent machine representations aligned with task constraints.



## **Knowledge–Skill Pairing**

This section maps the required knowledge areas to the observable skills that characterize successful performance of the competency.

### **Mapping Knowledge to Skills**

To demonstrate this competency, learners must be able to:

* **Create** computational solutions by applying **Turing Machine** concepts to model, implement, and validate problem-specific behaviors, such as data classification and counting, within a formal machine structure.

* **Apply** **Requirements Engineering** to identify and formalize system requirements, ensuring that stakeholder needs and problem constraints are accurately reflected in the constructed Turing Machine.

* **Apply** **Analytical and Critical Thinking (FPK)** to decompose the problem, select appropriate modeling strategies, and justify design decisions throughout the solution development process.



## **Bloom’s Taxonomy Alignment**

Each knowledge component is aligned with **Bloom’s Revised Taxonomy** to support structured assessment:

* **Turing Machines — Create**
  Assesses the ability to design and construct executable Turing Machines that solve the given problem.

* **Requirements Engineering — Apply**
  Evaluates the ability to interpret and structure system requirements into formal specifications.

* **Analytical and Critical Thinking (FPK) — Apply**
  Assesses the ability to analyze the problem context, decompose it into manageable components, and reason about solution strategies.



## **Verb Annotation**

The following action verbs define the expected performance:

* **Turing Machines — Create:** *Develop, Construct, Invent*
* **Requirements Engineering — Apply:** *Interpret, Organize, Apply*
* **Analytical and Critical Thinking (FPK) — Apply:** *Decompose, Identify, Apply*
