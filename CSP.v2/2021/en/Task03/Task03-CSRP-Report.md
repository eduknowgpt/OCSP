# Competency Specification Review Report: Task03 –  The Farmer Robot

## 1. Introduction

Building on the **Competency Specification Process (CSP) framework**, this report documents **Phase 2 – Competency Expert Review** for the PBL task “The Farmer Robot”. The purpose of this phase is to systematically and collaboratively validate the competency specifications—encompassing their knowledge, skills, and dispositions—for:

- Accuracy and theoretical soundness, reflecting domain expertise

- Clarity and internal consistency, ensuring precise and unambiguous competencies

- Pedagogical alignment, confirming relevance to intended learning outcomes

- Contextual applicability, evaluating real-world feasibility and educational value

The review draws on expert judgment and is supported by a structured set of evaluation criteria. Feedback was collected from experienced instructors through guided interviews, enabling a detailed appraisal of the competency elements—knowledge, skills, and dispositions—mapped to the task. This report synthesizes the findings and offers recommendations to improve the coherence, clarity, and instructional value of the competency specifications.

The findings follow the stages of the Competency Specification Protocol:

- Instructional-context analysis: Validating task complexity and competency requirements

- Item-level evaluation: Identifying strengths, inconsistencies, and areas needing clarification

- Compreensive synthesis: Integrating findings across clarity, accuracy, alignment, and relevance

- Recommendations: Offering concrete enhancements to optimize the competency model

By anchoring the review in Phase 2 of CSP, this report ensures that the competency specifications are theoretically rigorous, pedagogically robust, and educationally effective.

## 6. Recommendations Synthesis

Based on expert feedback and protocol standards, the following action-oriented recommendations are proposed to enhance the Task 03 competency framework:

- **Refine Knowledge Components**

   * Decompose broad topics into discrete, task-relevant units. (e.g., splitting “FSM theory” into “state-transition tables,” “minimization,” etc.).
   * Replace “Regular Languages” with “Regular Expressions” to match deliverable expectations.
   * Remove irrelevant topics such as the “Chomsky Hierarchy.”

- **Clarify Competency Titles & Descriptions**

   * Adopt precise, Bloom-aligned verbs (e.g., *Design*, *Construct*, *Formulate*) in place of generic terms like “Determine.”
   * Ensure each competency title clearly reflects the Task 03 scenario and desired learner outcomes.
   * Eliminate redundancy across competencies to maintain clear scope differentiation.

- **Align Knowledge–Skill Pairings with Bloom Levels**

   * Adjust cognitive levels to reflect task complexity (e.g., *Apply*, *Analyze*, *Create*).
   * Include brief justifications linking knowledge elements to expected competencies.

- **Enhance Learning Objective Specificity**

   * Exclude non-essential theoretical content such as the Church–Turing Thesis.



## 7. Conclusion & Decision

Implementing the above recommendations will significantly enhance the quality and instructional value of the competency specification for *Task 03 – The Farmer Robot*. By removing irrelevant theoretical content and refining the granularity of the knowledge items, the competencies become more directly aligned with the actual learning goals of the task.

Moreover, reformulated wording and better alignment with Bloom’s Taxonomy will provide clearer guidance for both instructors and learners. These improvements ensure that the competencies are **well-scoped, relevant, and clearly articulated**, which supports effective implementation and assessment.

As a next step, it is advised to incorporate the proposed adjustments into the official version of the competency specification and conduct a follow-up validation with the original or additional reviewers. This iterative process will further ensure the robustness and educational effectiveness of the final competency framework.
