# Expert Review – Cycle 2: Task 1 – *The Vending Machine for Sodas and Snacks*

A second review cycle was conducted following prior revisions, during which the reviewers **approved all enhancements** made to the Competency Specification.

However, during this cycle, the authors also proposed a **refinement to the terminology** used:

> The term **“Automata”**, while broadly accurate, was considered **too general** and potentially misleading. It could encompass advanced models such as Pushdown Automata, Turing Machines, or Linear Bounded Automata—**which are outside the scope** of Task 1.

To clarify the focus of the task, it was agreed that the competency descriptions would refer specifically to **“Finite Automata”** in place of the previously generic term. This ensures a precise alignment with the intended conceptual scope of designing and validating a vending machine model.

The agreed-upon changes have been incorporated and are detailed in the updated report: **Task01‑CSP‑Report‑rev1.md**.


### Key Highlights from Cycle 2

1. All previous edits to competencies—structural, linguistic, and Bloom‑level—were approved by the review panel.
2. The authors clarified the competency language by replacing “Automata” with **“Finite Automata”** to avoid ambiguity and scope creep.
3. The finalized competency specification, reflecting this change, is documented in *Task01‑CSP‑Report‑rev1.md*.

This refinement reinforces the precision and instructional alignment of the competency framework, ensuring the task remains well-defined and conceptually sound.
